Visit Alexandria

The Pearl of the Mediterranean

About the City

Alexandria was founded by Alexander the Great in 331 BC. It was home to the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, and to one of the greatest libraries of antiquity. Today it is Egypt's main Mediterranean port, known as the Pearl of the Mediterranean.

Read more about Alexandria on Wikipedia

Popular Attractions

Bibliotheca Alexandrina

A modern library and cultural center with museums and exhibitions.

Qaitbay Citadel

A 15th-century seaside fortress with amazing views of the Mediterranean.
